About Konakrikoin

Energy Today, A Better Tomorrow

Konakrikoin Gas & Oil Limited is a modern energy business based in Akwa Ibom State. We supply quality petroleum products, cooking gas (LPG), diesel, lubricants and related energy solutions to homes, transport operators, businesses and industry.

Our goal is simple: to be a trusted partner for safe, reliable and efficient energy supply — supporting everyday needs today while building capacity for tomorrow.

🛡️

Safe & Reliable

We prioritise product quality and careful handling so customers can depend on what they receive.

🤝

Professional Service

From retail customers to bulk buyers, we aim to respond promptly and clearly to every enquiry.

🌱

Sustainable Focus

We support practical energy solutions that help communities and businesses keep moving forward.

Our presence: We operate a station at 38 Idoro Road, Uyo, and are developing a new station at Enwang Road, Ibaka, Mbo LGA, to better serve the coastal communities of the state.

Whether you need cooking gas for the home, diesel for generators and fleets, lubricants, bulk delivery or procurement support, Konakrikoin is here to help.
